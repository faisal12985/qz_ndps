Academics

🎓 Primary School (Classes 1-5)

Foundation years focusing on basic literacy, numeracy, and social skills through interactive and play-based learning methods.

  • English, Mathematics, Science
  • Environmental Studies
  • Art and Craft
  • Physical Education

🎓 Middle School (Classes 6-8)

Building stronger academic foundations with specialized subject teachers and introduction to advanced concepts.

  • Core Subjects: English, Math, Science, Social Studies
  • Optional: Computer Science, Music, Dance
  • Library and Research Skills
  • Group Projects and Presentations

🎓 Coaching (Classes 9-12)

Comprehensive preparation for board examinations with stream specialization in Science, Commerce, and Arts.

  • Science Stream: Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Math
  • Commerce Stream: Accounts, Economics, Business Studies
  • Arts Stream: History, Geography, Political Science
  • Career Guidance and Counseling

🏆 Academic Excellence Programs

Remedial Classes

Competitive Exam Preparation

Coaching for JEE, NEET, and other competitive examinations.
